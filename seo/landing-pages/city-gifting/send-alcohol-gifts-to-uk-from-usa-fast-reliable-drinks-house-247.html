The simplest way to send an alcohol gift from the USA to the UK is not to ship it at all. Posting alcohol across the Atlantic means courier restrictions, customs forms and duty for the person receiving it. Instead, order from a UK retailer that already holds the stock here.

You order on our site, enter the recipient's UK address and add a message. We dispatch from our licensed unit in Battersea, so the gift never crosses a border: no international shipping, no customs and no duty for the person receiving it. Across Greater London we deliver 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes. Elsewhere in mainland UK, choose express (1–2 business days) or standard (3–5 business days) at checkout.

For a few weeks each spring and autumn the US and UK change their clocks on different dates, and the gap is an hour shorter. Because London deliveries run 24/7, even an order placed late in your afternoon still arrives the same London evening.

How to order from the USA

  1. Choose the gift. Champagne, whisky, wine, a hamper or flowers.
  2. Enter the UK address. Include the recipient's phone number so the driver can reach them.
  3. Add your message. Write it at checkout and we include it with the delivery. Gift wrap is available.
  4. Pay as you normally would. Prices and checkout are in pounds sterling. US cards work as normal, and your bank converts the charge to dollars.

Questions from the USA

Do I need a UK card or billing address?
No. Only the delivery address has to be in the UK. Your card and billing address can be where you are.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK with UK duty already paid, so nothing crosses a border.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. We check age on every alcohol delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Is the stock genuine?
Yes. Everything is bought from UK wholesalers approved under HMRC's Alcohol Wholesaler Registration Scheme, with UK duty paid. See authenticity and sourcing.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

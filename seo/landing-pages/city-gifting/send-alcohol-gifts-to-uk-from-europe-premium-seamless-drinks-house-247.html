Since Brexit, alcohol posted from the EU to the UK is liable to UK duty and VAT, and the recipient is often asked to pay before the parcel is handed over. Many couriers will not carry alcohol at all. Ordering from a UK retailer with the stock already here avoids both problems.

You order on our site, enter the recipient's UK address and add a message. We dispatch from our licensed unit in Battersea, so the gift never crosses a border: no international shipping, no customs and no duty for the person receiving it. Across Greater London we deliver 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes. Elsewhere in mainland UK, choose express (1–2 business days) or standard (3–5 business days) at checkout.

Questions from Europe

Why not post a bottle from home?
Alcohol sent from the EU to the UK is liable to UK duty and VAT, and many couriers refuse it. Our stock is already in London with UK duty paid.
Is the stock genuine?
Yes. Everything is bought from UK wholesalers approved under HMRC's Alcohol Wholesaler Registration Scheme, with UK duty paid. See authenticity and sourcing.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. We check age on every alcohol delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

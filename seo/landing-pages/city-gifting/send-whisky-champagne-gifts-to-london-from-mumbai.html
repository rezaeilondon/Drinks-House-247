Questions from Mumbai

Will my Indian card work?
Most Indian credit and debit cards work once international online payments are switched on in your banking app. Checkout is in pounds sterling.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. We check age on every alcohol delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK with UK duty already paid, so nothing crosses a border.

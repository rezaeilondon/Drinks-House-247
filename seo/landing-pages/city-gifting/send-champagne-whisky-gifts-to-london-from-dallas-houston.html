Energy, finance and law tie Texas closely to London, and plenty of Dallas and Houston firms have teams, partners and clients there. London is six hours ahead of Texas for most of the year. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes, so an order you place over breakfast can reach a London desk that afternoon.

Everything is dispatched in London. Nothing is shipped from the US, so there is no customs and no duty for the person receiving it.

Questions from Dallas and Houston

Can you deliver to an office?
Yes. Include the company name, floor and the recipient's phone number. Someone aged 18 or over must accept any delivery that contains alcohol, so make sure reception can sign for it.
Do I need a UK card or billing address?
No. Only the delivery address has to be in the UK. Your card and billing address can be where you are.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK with UK duty already paid, so nothing crosses a border.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. We check age on every alcohol delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

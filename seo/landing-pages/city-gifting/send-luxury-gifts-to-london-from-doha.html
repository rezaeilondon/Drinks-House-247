How to order from Doha

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Add your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your Qatari card; your bank converts the charge to riyals.

Questions from Doha

Can you deliver to a London hotel or serviced apartment?
Yes. Include the guest's full name and any flat or suite number, and note that the delivery is for a guest.
Can I mix alcohol-free items with champagne in one order?
Yes. If the order contains alcohol, someone aged 18 or over must accept it, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Do I need a UK billing address?
No. Only the delivery address has to be in the UK.

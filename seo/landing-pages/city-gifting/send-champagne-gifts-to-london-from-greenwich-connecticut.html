Plenty of Greenwich firms have partners, investors and clients in Mayfair and the City. When a deal closes or a colleague is promoted, a bottle on their desk the same day says more than an email. London is five hours ahead of Connecticut for most of the year, and our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

Everything is dispatched in London, so nothing is exported from the US. There is no customs, no duty and no paperwork for the person receiving it.

Greenwich time vs London time

Order in Greenwich Time in London What to expect
7am noon On their desk by lunchtime
11am 4pm Before the London office closes

Times assume the usual five-hour difference. For a few weeks each spring and autumn, when the US and UK change their clocks on different dates, the gap is four hours. Most offices stop receiving deliveries by early evening, so send later orders to a home address.

Questions from Greenwich

Can you deliver to an office in the City or Mayfair?
Yes. Include the company name, floor and the recipient's phone number. If the office has a post room or reception, they will usually sign for it — someone aged 18 or over must accept any delivery that contains alcohol.
Is the stock genuine?
Yes. Everything is bought from UK wholesalers approved under HMRC's Alcohol Wholesaler Registration Scheme, with UK duty paid. See authenticity and sourcing.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK with UK duty already paid, so nothing crosses a border.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

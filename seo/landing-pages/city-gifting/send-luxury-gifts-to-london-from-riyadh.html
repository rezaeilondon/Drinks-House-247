Riyadh is two hours ahead of London in the British summer and three hours ahead in winter, so the working days overlap well. Order in your afternoon and the gift reaches a London home, hotel or office by the end of their morning. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

Most senders from Riyadh choose alcohol-free gifts: hampers, caviar, chocolates and flowers. Everything is bought and dispatched in London, so nothing is shipped from Saudi Arabia.

How to order from Riyadh

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.

  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your card; your bank converts the charge to riyals.

Questions from Riyadh

Can I send to a London hotel?
Yes. Give the guest's full name and the hotel address, and note that the delivery is for a guest. Hotels usually receive it at reception or the concierge desk.
Can I send a gift for Eid or Ramadan?
Yes. The Eid Hamper is alcohol-free, and chocolates, caviar and flowers suit an iftar invitation or a family visit. Add your greeting at checkout.
Do alcohol-free gifts need an adult to sign?
Age checks apply to orders that contain alcohol. For those, someone aged 18 or over must accept the delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

Many Kuwaiti families have a son or daughter studying in London, or a flat in Knightsbridge they visit each summer. Kuwait is two hours ahead of London in the British summer and three hours ahead in winter, so a gift ordered in your afternoon arrives during their day. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

Most senders from Kuwait choose alcohol-free gifts. Everything is bought and dispatched in London, so nothing is shipped from Kuwait.

Questions from Kuwait City

Can I send a gift to a student in London?
Yes. Chocolates, flowers and non-alcoholic gifts can go to anyone. Orders containing alcohol can only be accepted by someone aged 18 or over, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Can I send to a London hotel?
Yes. Give the guest's full name and the hotel address, and note that the delivery is for a guest. Hotels usually receive it at reception or the concierge desk.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Will the recipient pay anything on delivery?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK.

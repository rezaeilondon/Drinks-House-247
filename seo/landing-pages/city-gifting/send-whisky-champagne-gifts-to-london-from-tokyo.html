Tokyo is eight hours ahead of London in the British summer and nine hours ahead in winter. Order during your working day and it would reach London before dawn, so the better habit is to order in the evening: what you choose after dinner in Tokyo arrives during London's morning or afternoon. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

The gift is dispatched from London, so there is no export from Japan, no customs and no duty for the recipient.

How to order from Tokyo

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Write your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your Japanese card; your card company converts the charge to yen.

Questions from Tokyo

Can you deliver to a company office in London?
Yes. Include the company name and a contact who is 18 or over. For several addresses at once, contact us.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. Under our Challenge 25 policy, anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Is everything genuine?
Yes. Stock comes from UK wholesalers approved under HMRC's Alcohol Wholesaler Registration Scheme. See authenticity and sourcing.

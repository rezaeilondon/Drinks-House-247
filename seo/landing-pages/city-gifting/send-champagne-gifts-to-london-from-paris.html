Paris is only an hour ahead of London all year round, so there is no time difference to plan around — just the Channel. Shipping a bottle from France means a courier, a wait of several days and paperwork for the person receiving it. Ordering from us means it leaves our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea and reaches a London door typically in 30–45 minutes, 24 hours a day.

France and the UK change their clocks on the same dates, so the gap is always one hour.

How to order from Paris

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Write your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your French card; your bank converts the charge to euros.

Questions from Paris

Why not just bring a bottle on the Eurostar?
You can, if you are travelling. Ordering from us means it arrives on the day you choose without you being there, gift-wrapped and with a message.
Will the recipient pay any duty or customs?
No. UK duty is already paid, and the order never crosses a border.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. Under our Challenge 25 policy, anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

Sydney is between nine and eleven hours ahead of London, depending on the time of year, which makes the timing of a gift almost the opposite of what you would expect. Order in the evening in Sydney and it arrives in London's morning. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes, so you can choose the moment.

Nothing is shipped from Australia. The gift is dispatched in London, so there is no customs, no duty and no fortnight in the post.

From November to March the gap is eleven hours, and from April to September it is nine. For a few weeks in October and in late March to early April, when only one country is on daylight saving, it is ten.

How to order from Sydney

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Add your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your Australian card; your bank converts the charge to dollars.

Questions from Sydney

Can I send to family outside London?
Yes. We deliver across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout. Allow for that around Christmas.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. Under our Challenge 25 policy, anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK with UK duty already paid.
Do I need a UK address for billing?
No. Only the delivery address has to be in the UK.

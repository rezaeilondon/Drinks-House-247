Everything is dispatched from London, so nothing is shipped from California: no customs forms, no duty, and no bottle sitting in a depot for a week.

Questions from Los Angeles

Is a delivery to a London hotel possible?
Yes. Put the guest's full name and the hotel address, and add a note that the delivery is for a guest. Hotels can usually receive it at reception or the concierge desk.
Is everything genuine?
Yes. Everything is bought from UK wholesalers approved under HMRC's Alcohol Wholesaler Registration Scheme, with UK duty paid. Read more about authenticity and sourcing.
Who can sign for it?
Anyone aged 18 or over. Under our Challenge 25 policy, anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for ID.
Can you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.

London runs five hours ahead of New York for most of the year. That gap is usually what makes sending a gift across the Atlantic awkward: by the time you think of it at the end of a Manhattan workday, it is already late evening in London. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes, so a bottle you order from your desk can be at their door the same night.

You order on our site from New York, enter the recipient's UK address and add a message. We deliver locally from London, so the bottle never crosses a border: no international shipping, no customs and no duty for the person receiving it.

How it works from New York

  1. Choose the gift. Champagne, whisky, wine or a hamper. Every order is dispatched from our licensed unit in London.
  2. Enter the UK address. Include the recipient's phone number so the driver can reach them.
  3. Add your message. Write it at checkout and we include it with the delivery. Gift wrap is available too.
  4. Pay as you normally would. Prices and checkout are in pounds sterling. Cards issued in the US work as normal, and your bank converts the charge to dollars.

Sending outside London

We deliver across mainland UK as well, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout. If the recipient is in Edinburgh, Manchester or the Cotswolds rather than London, allow for that when you order.

Questions from New York

Do I need a UK card or a UK billing address?
No. Only the delivery address needs to be in the UK. Your card and billing address can be in the US.
Will the recipient pay customs or duty?
No. The gift is dispatched from our licensed unit in London, so it never leaves the UK.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. We check age on every alcohol delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for ID.
Can I choose when it arrives?
London orders go out as soon as they are placed. If you want it to land at a particular moment — the morning of a birthday, say — time your order with the table above, or contact us first.

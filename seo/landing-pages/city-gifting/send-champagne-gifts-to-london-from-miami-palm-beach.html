Plenty of people split the year between Florida and London, and the friends and family left on the other side still have birthdays. London is five hours ahead of Miami and Palm Beach for most of the year, so a gift ordered over breakfast in Florida reaches a London address in the early afternoon. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

You order from Florida; we deliver from London. Nothing is shipped across the Atlantic, so there is no customs, no duty and no wait.

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with a phone number for the recipient.

  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your usual card; your bank converts the amount to dollars.

Questions from Florida

Can I send to someone outside London?
Yes. We deliver across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Can I send a gift to my own London home before I arrive?
Yes, as long as someone aged 18 or over is there to receive it. We check age on every alcohol delivery under our Challenge 25 policy.
Are there customs charges for the recipient?
None. The order is fulfilled inside the UK.
How do I know it arrived?
You receive email confirmation of the order, and London deliveries usually arrive within 30–45 minutes of dispatch.

Toronto has deep family ties to Britain, and that usually means someone on the other side of the Atlantic with a birthday you cannot be there for. London is five hours ahead of Toronto for most of the year. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes, so you can order after work in Toronto and it still arrives the same evening in London.

The gift is dispatched from London rather than shipped from Canada, so there are no customs forms, no duty and no week-long wait.

How to order from Toronto

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Add your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your Canadian card; your bank converts the charge to dollars.

Questions from Toronto

Do I need a UK billing address?
No. Only the delivery address has to be in the UK.
Can you deliver to family outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Who can accept the delivery?
Someone aged 18 or over. Under our Challenge 25 policy, anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Will the recipient pay anything on delivery?
No. UK duty is already paid and the order never leaves the UK.

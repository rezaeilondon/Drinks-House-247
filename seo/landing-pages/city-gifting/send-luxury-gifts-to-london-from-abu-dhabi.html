Abu Dhabi is three hours ahead of London in the British summer and four hours ahead in winter, so the working days overlap well: order in your afternoon and the gift reaches a London address by late morning. Our licensed dispatch unit in Battersea delivers across Greater London 24 hours a day, typically in 30–45 minutes.

Not every recipient drinks, and not every sender wants to send alcohol. Our gift range includes alcohol-free hampers, caviar, chocolates and flowers alongside champagne and fine spirits, so you can choose what suits the person and the occasion. Everything is dispatched in London, so nothing is shipped from the UAE.

How to order from Abu Dhabi

  1. Choose the gift and enter the UK delivery address, with the recipient's phone number.
  2. Add your message at checkout. Gift wrap is available.
  3. Pay in pounds sterling with your UAE card; your bank converts the charge to dirhams.

Questions from Abu Dhabi

Can I send to a hotel in London?
Yes. Give the guest's full name and the hotel address, and note that the delivery is for a guest. Hotels usually receive it at reception or the concierge desk.
Do alcohol-free gifts need an adult to sign?
Age checks apply to orders that contain alcohol. For those, someone aged 18 or over must accept the delivery, and under our Challenge 25 policy anyone who looks under 25 will be asked for photo ID.
Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, across mainland UK, with express (1–2 business days) and standard (3–5 business days) options shown at checkout.
Will the recipient pay anything on delivery?
No. The order is fulfilled inside the UK.

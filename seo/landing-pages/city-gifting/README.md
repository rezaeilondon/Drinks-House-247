# City gifting landing pages

Twelve "send a gift to London from <city>" pages for overseas senders, published 2026-10-01.
`pages.json` holds the Shopify page IDs, handles and SEO title/description; each `<handle>.html` is the live body.

Linked from the existing country pages (a "Sending from ...?" section appended at the end of each):

| Country page | City guides linked |
|---|---|
| USA | New York, Beverly Hills & LA, Miami & Palm Beach, San Francisco |
| Canada | Toronto |
| Australia | Sydney |
| Europe | Paris, Monaco |
| China / Hong Kong | Hong Kong |
| Singapore | Hong Kong, Tokyo |
| Saudi Arabia | Abu Dhabi, Doha |
| UAE / Dubai | Abu Dhabi, Doha |

Facts used on every page: licensed dispatch unit in Battersea (not a shop), London delivery 24/7 typically
30–45 minutes, mainland UK express 1–2 / standard 3–5 business days, checkout in GBP, stock from HMRC
AWRS-approved UK wholesalers with UK duty paid, Challenge 25.

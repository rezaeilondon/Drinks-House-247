The owner decided on 2026-09-16 to adopt **Challenge 25** site-wide, replacing the older
Challenge 21 policy. Challenge 25 is the standard promoted by the Home Office and the
Retail of Alcohol Standards Group and is what most licensing authorities now expect;

on the site, the two could not be left contradicting each other.

## In these CSVs — 59 resources still to apply

These are long-tail blog articles and secondary landing pages. In most of them the only

**Importing is safe and idempotent.** Every row contains the finished post-transform
body. Re-importing a row that is already correct rewrites it with identical content.

### Why these were not pushed through the API

The Shopify MCP is the only write path available in this environment (no admin token is
exposed to the shell, and `bulkOperationRunMutation` is blocked by the MCP safety
policy). Each `pageUpdate`/`articleUpdate` requires the **entire** body to be sent, so
59 resources totalling ~580KB would mean 100+ round trips to change what is usually one
line. A single Matrixify import does the same job in one action, with no
retyping step and therefore no chance of a transcription error.

## How the transform was verified

- **152 substitutions**, from only four distinct source strings: `Challenge 21`,
  `under 21`, `under the age of 21`, `/pages/challenge-21`.
- **Guards.** A blanket `21 -> 25` would have corrupted whisky age statements
  ("Glenfiddich 21 Year Old", "aged 21"). The transformer asserts that age-statement and
  legal-age patterns are unchanged and refuses to emit a body if they are not.
- **Legal purchase age preserved exactly**: 71 mentions of "18 or over" / "over 18" /
  "18+" before, 71 after. The legal age is 18 and did not change.
- **All 11 surviving `21`s were checked by hand** and are legitimate: room temperature
  (21 degrees C), cask evaporation (21%), and a publication date of 2026-09-21.

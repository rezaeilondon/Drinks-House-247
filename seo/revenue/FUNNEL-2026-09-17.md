Measured from Shopify analytics and the Admin API, not estimated.

|---|---|

## The funnel, and where it breaks

| Step | Sessions | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | 29,147 | — |
| Added to cart | 590 | **2.0%** |
| Reached checkout | 423 | 71.7% of carts |
| **Completed checkout** | **135** | **31.9% of checkouts** |
| Overall conversion | | **0.46%** |

Two distinct leaks, and they need different fixes:

1. **98% of visitors never add anything to a cart.** This is the deepest leak by volume
   but the slowest to fix — it is a product-page and pricing problem.
2. **68% of people who reach checkout do not finish.** This is the recoverable one.
   These people chose a product, entered the checkout, and stopped. A healthy store
   converts 50–70% at this step.

## Abandoned checkouts: the single biggest recoverable number

**128 abandoned checkouts in 90 days.** Measured from a 50-record sample:

| | |
|---|---|
| Average abandoned basket | £130.02 |
| Estimated total abandoned | **£16,642** |
| Of those, with an email address captured | 78% |
| **Estimated recoverable value** | **£12,981** |

That is **61% of realised revenue** sitting in baskets that were filled and left.

Recovery at industry-typical rates:

| Recovery rate | Revenue | Orders |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | £649 | ~5 |
| 10% | £1,298 | ~10 |
| 15% | £1,947 | ~15 |

Shopify sends abandoned-checkout emails automatically **if the setting is on**. Every
abandoned checkout already carries a working `abandonedCheckoutUrl` that restores the
basket in one click. If that setting is off, this is free money that has been sitting
untouched for 90 days.

## Email is an unused channel

Traffic by source over 90 days:

| Source | Sessions | Orders |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | 17,467 | 96 |
| Search | 11,452 | 37 |
| Social | 100 | 0 |
| **Email** | **1** | **0** |

**One session from email in 90 days.** For a 24-hour alcohol delivery business — which is
inherently repeat-purchase — this is the largest unexploited asset in the account. There
are 171 orders' worth of customers plus ~100 captured abandoned-checkout addresses.

Note: `customersCount` could not be used to size the list. Its query filter is silently
ignored (a nonsense filter returns the same number) and the value returned, 10,000, is a
ceiling rather than a count. The list size needs reading from the admin.

## Mobile converts worse than desktop at checkout

| Device | Sessions | Reached checkout | Completed | Completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop | 16,721 | 205 | 78 | **38.0%** |
| Mobile | 12,326 | 215 | 56 | **26.0%** |

Mobile is 42% of traffic and *more* people reach checkout on mobile than desktop — but a
third fewer finish. Something in the mobile checkout is losing people. Worth walking
through a real purchase on a phone.

## Order of attack

1. **Confirm abandoned-checkout recovery emails are enabled.** Settings → Checkout.
   Biggest return, zero cost, already-built.
2. **Walk a mobile purchase end to end.** 12,326 sessions a quarter are hitting the
   weaker path.
3. **Email the existing customer base.** The channel is at literally one session.
4. **Then** work on the 2% cart-add rate, which is the long game.

# Drinks House 247: story series bible

## Rules for every episode
- **Format:** 45 seconds, made from 3 × 15s clips, vertical 9:16.
- **Cast:** the mascot is in the DH247 uniform (navy polo, navy cap with a small gold crown). Every other person looks clearly over 25.
- **Drinking:** nobody drinks on camera. Alcohol is never shown changing mood, confidence, romance or social success; the emotion comes from the gesture of the gift.
- **Delivery:** the rider hands the bag to an adult and checks ID on camera.
- **Labels:** use real products from the photo references. Keep labels at chest height or further, with no label close-ups.
- **Speech:** write hard words phonetically in the prompt (e.g. "Mo-WETT", "Peh-ree-NYON").
- **End card:** "Next chapter →" plus a teaser line. Add the disclaimer "18+ only. Please drink responsibly." and the DH247 logo.
- **Zodiac framing:** zodiac content is light-hearted personality lore ("they say Scorpios..."), never fortune-telling or advice.

## QA checklist for the Chrome reviewing agents
1. **Labels:** is a bottle or box label garbled, misspelt or with invented text? Note the timestamp.
2. **Pronunciation:** are the product names correct (Moët, Pol Roger, Hibiki, Laphroaig, Ruinart...)?
3. **Lip-sync:** is it off by more than about 0.5 seconds?
4. **Hands and objects:** look for warped hands, objects melting or bottles changing shape.
5. **Rules:** check that nobody drinks, there is no visible third-party logo or crest, and nobody looks under 25.
6. **Story:** is the hook clear in the first 2 seconds? Is there a payoff at the end?
7. **Verdict:** KEEP / FIX (give a new prompt note) / REDO.

## Series A: "The Gift Whisperer" (zodiac, 12 episodes)
The mascot is London's gift detective. Each episode a mystery customer of one star sign sends a cryptic order, and he cracks what that sign would love. Each episode ends with a new note that sets up the next sign.

- **Ep1 Scorpio:** "Surprise me. If you can." Krug Grande Cuvée & Caviar Hamper. Teaser: "Next: my Sagittarius sister"
- **Ep2 Sagittarius** (season starts 22 Nov): adventurous, so a gift "from somewhere else": Great British Hamper / Clase Azul set
- **Ep3 Capricorn:** status and quality: Dom Pérignon Prestige Hamper
- **Ep4 Aquarius:** unusual and arty: Hirst / Murakami Prestige Hamper
- *(Continues through the year in zodiac order.)*

## Series B: "What the Gift Said" (psychology of gifting)
One couple, Sam and Alex, told through the gifts they send each other. Each episode is one emotion carried by a gift:
1. sorry
2. thank you
3. I miss you
4. I'm proud of you
5. let's start again

Each episode ends unresolved: "Did Alex open it?"

## Series C: "Night Shift 247" (rider stories)
One rider, one London night, one doorstep story per episode: a proposal planned at Tower Bridge, two brothers making up, a nurse coming home after a night shift. The mascot is the dispatcher on the radio.

## Series D: "The Hamper That Travelled"
One gift bag is passed on across London boroughs (re-gifted). Each episode follows the next owner; the finale returns it to the first sender.

### Shot 1 (0–15s): The note
A dark DH247 dispatch office at night, with neon "24/7" glow and rain on the window. The mascot, in uniform, reads an order on a tablet; on-screen text reads "Surprise me. If you can. — S."

He narrows his eyes: "A Scorpio. Born late October. Intense... private... sees straight through you. One wrong gift and you're out of their life."

He pins the note to a corkboard with a zodiac wheel and red string.

### Shot 2 (15–30s): The choice
He walks along shelves of hampers, rejecting the flashy ones with a shake of the head: "Nothing loud. A Scorpio wants depth. Something rare... with a secret inside."

He lifts the Krug & caviar hamper (reference) and places it in the cream DH247 gift bag (reference). He writes a card, then seals it with a wink.

### Shot 3 (30–45s): The doorstep
A Kensington townhouse door at night. The rider, in a navy DH247 jacket, checks the photo ID of a woman in her 30s dressed in a black coat, then hands her the gift bag.

She reads the card and a slow smile appears.

VO (mascot): "They say Scorpios never forget a gift... or the person who got it right."

She turns the card over. On the back, handwritten: "Not bad. Now try my Sagittarius sister."
